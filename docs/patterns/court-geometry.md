# Court Geometry & Shot Selection

**The Court is a Geometry Problem. Solve It.**

---

## The Five Zones

| Zone | Location | Strategy | Shot Selection |
|------|----------|----------|----------------|
| **1. Deep Behind Baseline** | 3m+ behind | **Defend** | High cross-court, lob, deep slice |
| **2. On Baseline** | 0-3m behind | **Neutralize** | Deep cross-court, heavy spin |
| **3. Inside Baseline (No Man's Land)** | Baseline to service line | **Transition** | Drive approach, drop shot, short angle |
| **4. Service Line to Net** | Service line to 3m from net | **Attack** | Approach volley, put-away volley |
| **5. At Net** | 0-3m from net | **Finish** | Angle volley, overhead, drop volley |

---

## The Golden Rule

> **Your position on court dictates your shot selection. Not your preference. Not your ego. Your POSITION.**

| If You're Here... | You Hit This... |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Deep (Zone 1) | High, deep, cross-court. **Never** go for winners. |
| Baseline (Zone 2) | Deep cross-court. Build. Wait for short. |
| Transition (Zone 3) | **Decision point.** Short = attack. Deep = reset. |
| Approaching (Zone 4) | Deep approach → split → volley. |
| Net (Zone 5) | Angle, put away, overhead. Finish. |

## The Decision Tree

```
BALL COMES TO YOU
       │
       ▼
WHERE AM I?
       │
       ├── ZONE 1 (Deep) ──→ HIGH CROSS / LOB / DEEP SLICE
       │
       ├── ZONE 2 (Baseline) ──→ DEEP CROSS / HEAVY SPIN
       │       │
       │       └── SHORT BALL? ──→ ZONE 3 (Transition)
       │
       ├── ZONE 3 (Transition) ──→ ATTACK or RESET?
       │       │
       │       ├── TIME + BALANCE ──→ DRIVE APPROACH / DROP SHOT
       │       │
       │       └── RUSHED / OFF BALANCE ──→ DEEP SLICE / LOB / RESET
       │
       ├── ZONE 4 (Approach) ──→ DEEP APPROACH → SPLIT → VOLLEY
       │
       └── ZONE 5 (Net) ──→ ANGLE / PUT AWAY / OVERHEAD
```

## Court Positioning: The Recovery Bisector

### The Rule
> **Recover to the bisector of the angle of possible returns.**

### Geometry
```
        OPPONENT
           ●
          / \
         /   \
        /     \
       /       \
      /         \
     /           \
    ●─────────────●
   YOU          TARGET
```

**Your recovery spot = midpoint of the angle their racket can reach.**

### Practical Recovery Spots

| You Hit To... | You Recover To... |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Cross-court deep** | Center of your half, slightly cross-court side |
| **Down the line** | Center of your half, slightly down-line side |
| **Middle** | Exact center of your half |
| **Short angle** | Inside baseline, toward that side |
| **Drop shot** | Inside baseline, ready to sprint forward |

## Shot Selection by Court Position

### From Zone 1 (Deep Defense)
| Incoming Ball | Your Shot | Target | Why |
|---------------|-----------|--------|-----|
| Hard & deep | High cross-court | Deep corner | Time to recover, high margin |
| High & deep | Lob | Over backhand | Reset, buy time |
| Slice | Slice back | Deep cross | Neutralize, stay in point |

### From Zone 2 (Baseline Neutral)
| Incoming Ball | Your Shot | Target | Why |
|---------------|-----------|--------|-----|
| Rally ball | Heavy cross-court | Deep corner | Move them, wait for short |
| Short (< baseline) | **ATTACK** | See Zone 3 | Opportunity |
| Wide | Run around → FH inside-out | Their BH | Dictate with weapon |

### From Zone 3 (Transition - The Decision Zone)
| Ball Characteristic | Decision | Shot |
|---------------------|----------|------|
| **Short, sit up, time** | ATTACK | Drive approach (FH push/BH saber) |
| **Short, low, rushed** | ATTACK CAREFULLY | Slice approach / drop shot |
| **Deep, pushing you back** | RESET | Deep cross-court / lob |
| **Wide, stretching** | DEFEND | High cross / lob / slice |

### From Zone 4 (Approach)
| Approach Type | Target | Next Move |
|---------------|--------|-----------|
| FH Drive | Deep to weak side | Split step → volley |
| BH Saber | Deep to weak side | Split step → volley |
| Slice | Deep, low, skidding | Split step → volley |
| Drop shot | Just over net, dies | Sprint in → put away |

### From Zone 5 (Net)
| Incoming | Response | Target |
|----------|----------|--------|
| High volley | Put away | Angle / open court |
| Low volley | Firm push deep | Deep / at feet |
| Half volley | Block deep | Deep middle |
| Lob | Overhead | Angle / at body |
| Body shot | Reflex block | Deep / angle |

## Target Zones (Where to Aim)

### The High-Percentage Targets
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│           DEEP CROSS (A)            │  ← 70% of shots
│         ┌───────────────┐           │
│         │               │           │
│         │    DEEP       │           │
│         │    MIDDLE     │           │  ← 20% of shots
│         │      (B)      │           │
│         │               │           │
│         └───────────────┘           │
│        SHORT ANGLE (C)              │  ← 10% of shots
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Target | When | Margin | Purpose |
|--------|------|--------|---------|
| **A: Deep Cross** | Baseline rally, defense, neutral | High (3m+ net clearance) | Move opponent, open court, time to recover |
| **B: Deep Middle** | Under pressure, wide ball, serve return | Highest | Neutralize, take away angles, force them to create |
| **C: Short Angle** | Short ball, opponent back, finishing | Low (net clearance 1m) | Pull off court, finish point |

---

## The "Middle" Rule

> **When in doubt, hit deep middle.**

| Situation | Why Middle Works |
|-----------|------------------|
| Return of serve | Takes away angles, forces them to generate pace |
| Wide ball | Recovers court position, neutralizes |
| Under pressure | Highest margin, lowest error |
| Mixed doubles / doubles | Confusion, "yours/mine" |
| Windy conditions | Safest target |

## Geometry of the Serve

| Serve Target | Opens | Follow-up Geometry |
|--------------|-------|-------------------|
| **Wide (Deuce)** | Ad court | Inside-out FH to open Deuce |
| **T (Deuce)** | Deuce court | Inside-in FH down line |
| **Body (Ad)** | Middle | Attack weak reply |
| **Wide (Ad)** | Deuce court | Run around → Inside-out FH |

---

## Geometry of the Return

| Serve Comes To... | Return Target | Why |
|-------------------|---------------|-----|
| **Wide** | Cross-court deep | High margin, follows ball angle |
| **T** | Deep middle / cross-court | Neutralize, take away angle |
| **Body** | Block deep middle | Survive, get in point |
| **Second serve** | Attack target (weak side) | Pressure, dictate |

## The Court Geometry Quick-Scan

**POSITION → ZONE → SHOT → TARGET → RECOVER**

| You Are | Zone | Default Shot | Default Target | Recover To |
|---------|------|--------------|----------------|------------|
| 3m+ back | 1 | High cross / lob | Deep cross (A) | Bisector |
| On baseline | 2 | Heavy cross | Deep cross (A) | Bisector |
| Inside baseline | 3 | **Attack or Reset** | Short angle (C) / Deep middle (B) | Bisector / Net |
| Service line | 4 | Approach deep | Deep weak side (A) | Net (split) |
| At net | 5 | Angle / put away | Open court | Net (split) |
