# Singles Patterns

**Five Core Patterns. Infinite Variations.**

---

## The Five Core Patterns

| Pattern | Name | When | CORE Keys |
|---------|------|------|-----------|
| **1** | **Serve + 1 Forehand** | Serve to weakness, dictate with FH | Q, ROOT, 8, BRAKE |
| **2** | **Serve + 1 Backhand** | Serve to BH, attack the return | V, ROOT, SABER, PULSE |
| **3** | **Forehand Dictation** | Run around BH, own the court | Q, 8, 45°, BRAKE |
| **4** | **Backhand Redirect** | Absorb pace, change direction | HUA, ROOT, SABER |
| **5** | **Transition/Finish** | Short ball → approach → volley | Q, ROOT, VOLLEY, PULSE |

---

## Pattern 1: Serve + 1 Forehand

**The Bread & Butter.**

### Serve Targets
| Target | Effect | Follow-up |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Wide (deuce)** | Opens court, forces stretch | Inside-out FH to open court |
| **T (deuce)** | Jams returner, weak reply | Inside-in FH down the line |
| **Body (ad)** | Handcuffs, floating return | Attack short ball with FH |
| **Wide (ad)** | Opens BH side | Run around, inside-out FH |

### The Sequence
1. **Serve** → Land target, hold Q on toss
2. **Split** → Read return direction at contact
3. **Move** → First step to FH side (even on BH returns)
4. **Load** → Coil, feel spiral line stretch
5. **Execute** → Park, 8, Brake, Pulse
6. **Recover** → Instant Hư, back to center

### Coach's Note
> **Run around everything.** If the return lets you hit a forehand, hit a forehand. Your FH push system is your weapon. The BH saber is your shield.

---

## Pattern 2: Serve + 1 Backhand

**When they protect the FH.**

### Serve Targets
| Target | Effect | Follow-up |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **T (ad)** | Jams FH, forces BH return | Saber down the line |
| **Body (deuce)** | Crowds FH, weak BH reply | Saber cross-court or DTL |
| **Wide (ad)** | Pulls them wide, short return | Step in, saber attack |

### The Sequence
1. **Serve** → Target BH side
2. **Read** → Return to your BH
3. **Load** → Non-dominant hand pulls racket high, feel lat stretch
4. **Execute** → Park, saber draw, edge leads, brake side-on, pulse
5. **Recover** → Back to center

---

## Pattern 3: Forehand Dictation

**The rally pattern. You own the court with your FH.**

### The Rules
1. **Run around BH** whenever time permits
2. **Inside-out** = control, margin, opens court
3. **Inside-in** = surprise, finishes points
4. **Cross-court** = build, move them, wait for short
5. **Never hit BH if you can hit FH** (unless defensive)

### Court Geometry
| Your Position | Their Position | Your Shot |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| FH corner | Center | Inside-out to BH |
| FH corner | BH corner | Inside-in to FH |
| Center | Center | Inside-out or cross |
| BH corner | FH corner | **Run around → Inside-out** |

### The Trigger
> **Short ball (lands inside baseline) = ATTACK.**
> **Deep ball = BUILD with cross-court FH.**

---

## Pattern 4: Backhand Redirect

**Hua Jin in action. Use their pace.**

### When to Redirect
- Fast ball to your BH
- You're on defense
- They're attacking your BH
- You want to change direction without generating pace

### The Technique
1. **Short takeback** (non-dominant hand pulls)
2. **Absorb** - let ball come to racket
3. **Face angle change** - top hand guides face to new target
4. **No swing** - body rotates slightly, racket redirects
5. **Pulse** - firm 1-inch guide

### Targets
| Incoming | Redirect To | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Cross-court BH | Down the line | Changes direction, wrong-foots |
| Down the line | Cross-court deep | Buys time, high margin |
| High BH | Offensive lob / saber drive | Attack the high ball |

---

## Pattern 5: Transition & Finish

**Short ball = opportunity. Don't just hit it - finish it.**

### The Sequence
1. **Recognize** → Ball lands short (inside baseline)
2. **Move** → Sprint to contact point, not where ball was
3. **Load** → Compact, early, feel stretch
4. **Execute** → Approach shot (drive or slice)
5. **Split** → At YOUR contact, split step
6. **Volley** → Read their reply, step & pulse
7. **Overhead** → If lob, track, move, brake, pulse

### Approach Shot Selection
| Ball Height | Approach Type | Target |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Waist + | Drive (FH push / BH saber) | Deep corner |
| Knee - Waist | Drive with heavy spin | Deep, kicking |
| Below knee | Slice approach | Deep, low, skidding |

### The Rule
> **Approach → Split → Volley → Put away. Never approach and watch.**

## Pattern Combinations

| Situation | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary |
|-----------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Serving** | S+1 FH | S+1 BH | Transition |
| **Returning** | Redirect | Block return | Chip & charge |
| **Rally (FH side)** | Dictate FH | Redirect BH | Transition |
| **Rally (BH side)** | Redirect BH | Run around FH | Defend deep |
| **Defense** | High margin cross | Lob | Redirect |

---

## The Singles Quick-Scan

**SERVE: Target → Split → Move → Load → PARK → 8 → BRAKE → PULSE → RECOVER**

**RALLY: Build cross → Wait short → Run around → Dictate FH → Transition → Finish**

**DEFEND: Absorb (Hua) → Redirect → Reset → Counter**
