# Tennis Fitness

**Train the Chain. Not the Muscles.**

---

## The Principle

> **Tennis fitness isn't general fitness. It's specific capacity for the Q-V-ROOT-8-45-Impulse-Jin chain.**

Every exercise must answer: *Does this improve my chain?*

---

## The Physical Demands of the Chain

| Variable | Physical Requirement | Training Focus |
|----------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Q** | Visual processing speed, focus endurance | Vision drills, cognitive load |
| **V** | Neck stability, vestibular tolerance | Neck isometrics, gaze stability |
| **ROOT** | Foot intrinsic strength, ankle stiffness, hip rotation | Barefoot work, single-leg, rotational power |
| **F** | Fascial elasticity, line continuity | Dynamic stretching, plyometrics, loaded carries |
| **P** | Proprioceptive acuity, spindle sensitivity | Unstable surfaces, eyes-closed, perturbation |
| **T** | Tensegrity integrity, no energy leaks | Core stiffness, anti-rotation, breathing |
| **SSC** | Elastic power, short amortization | Plyometrics, depth jumps, reactive hops |
| **8** | Coordinated continuous movement | Complex patterns, flow drills |
| **45°** | Diagonal plane strength | Rotational med ball, cable chops |
| **BRAKE** | Eccentric strength, deceleration | Eccentric overload, landing control |
| **PULSE** | Rate of force development | Ballistic intent, minimal time |
| **JIN** | Whole-body elastic expression | Integrated patterns, variability |

## The Training Pillars

### 1. Foot & Ankle Complex (ROOT Foundation)
| Exercise | Sets/Reps | Chain Link |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Single-leg balance** (eyes open/closed) | 3×30s | ROOT, P, V |
| **Arch lift + screw** (standing) | 3×10/side | ROOT, F |
| **Pogo hops** (stiff ankle) | 3×20s | SSC, ROOT |
| **Single-leg RDL** (barefoot) | 3×8/side | ROOT, F, T, P |
| **Copenhagen plank** | 3×20s/side | ROOT, T, F (adductor line) |

### 2. Hip & Torso Separation (8, 45°, BRAKE)
| Exercise | Sets/Reps | Chain Link |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Med ball rotational throws** | 4×6/side | 8, 45°, BRAKE, PULSE |
| **Cable/band chops & lifts** | 3×10/side | 45°, F (spiral/back line) |
| **Pallof press variations** | 3×10/side | T, BRAKE |
| **Russian twists (controlled)** | 3×12/side | 8, F |
| **Landmine rotations** | 3×8/side | 8, 45°, BRAKE |

### 3. Elastic Power (SSC, PULSE, JIN)
| Exercise | Sets/Reps | Chain Link |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Depth jumps** (low box) | 4×5 | SSC, BRAKE, PULSE |
| **Reactive lateral bounds** | 4×6/side | SSC, ROOT, 8 |
| **Split-step + sprint** | 6×10m | Q, ROOT, SSC |
| **Med ball slam + catch** | 4×6 | BRAKE, PULSE, T |
| **Band-resisted sprints** | 6×15m | PULSE, ROOT |

### 4. Upper Chain Integration (F, P, T, JIN)
| Exercise | Sets/Reps | Chain Link |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Push-up to side plank flow** | 3×8/side | F (spiral), T, P |
| **Single-arm landmine press** | 3×8/side | F, PULSE, 45° |
| **Band pull-apart + rotation** | 3×15 | F (back line), P, T |
| **Bottom-up KB carry** | 3×30m/side | P, T, F, ROOT |
| **Tennis-specific shadow + band** | 5×30s | Full chain integration |

### 5. Vision & Vestibular (Q, V)
| Drill | Duration | Chain Link |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Saccade training** (near-far) | 2×30s | Q |
| **Smooth pursuit** (tracking) | 2×30s | Q, V |
| **VOR cancellation** (thumb tracking + head turn) | 2×30s | V |
| **Gaze stability** (fixate + head move) | 2×30s | V, Q |
| **Strobe glasses** (if available) | During drills | Q, P |

| Day | Focus | Session |
|-----|-------|---------|

| **Tue** | **Strength + Chain Integration** | Hip/torso separation + upper chain + shadow |
| **Wed** | **Recovery / Mobility** | Fascial release, dynamic stretch, light feet |
| **Thu** | **Elastic Power + Vision** | Different plyos + med ball + Q/V |
| **Fri** | **Strength + Chain Integration** | Different patterns + upper chain + shadow |

| **Sun** | **Off** | Mental visualization, light walk |

## Off-Season Progression

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Volume |
|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| **Base** | 1-4 | Structural balance, tendon prep, movement quality | 3×/week, moderate |
| **Strength** | 5-8 | Max strength in chain patterns | 3×/week, heavy |
| **Power** | 9-12 | Rate of force development, SSC | 3×/week, explosive |
| **Specific** | 13-16 | Tennis-specific integration | 2×/week + court |

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Test | Target |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Single-leg hop** | CMJ (countermovement jump) | >45cm (M), >35cm (W) |
| **Lateral bound** | Triple hop for distance | >6m (M), >5m (W) |
| **Med ball throw** | Rotational throw distance | >15m (M), >12m (W) |
| **Reactive strength** | Drop jump RSI (height/time) | >2.0 |
| **Core stiffness** | Pallof press hold | 30s/side no rotation |
| **Vision** | Saccade speed / pursuit gain | Improving weekly |

## Periodization Quick Reference

| Phase | Strength | Power | Endurance | Tennis |
|-------|----------|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Off-season (Base)** | High | Low | Moderate | Low (technique) |
| **Pre-season (Strength)** | Very High | Low | Low | Moderate |
| **Pre-comp (Power)** | Moderate | Very High | Low | High |
| **In-season (Maintain)** | Low-Moderate | High | Match play | Very High |
| **Taper (Peak)** | Low | High (intent) | Very Low | Match ready |
