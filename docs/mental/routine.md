# Pre-Point Routine

**15 Seconds. Every Point. No Exceptions.**

---

## The Principle

> **The routine IS the trigger. It tells your nervous system: "Chain mode. Automatic. Trust it."**

No routine = conscious control = broken chain.

---

## The 15-Second Protocol

| Second | Action | Chain Variable |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **0-3** | **FEET** - Step to position. Feel tripods. Screw feet. Arch lift. | ROOT |
| **3-5** | **HEAD** - Chin tuck. Eyes level. Head still. | V |
| **5-7** | **HANDS** - Grip 3/10. Racket throat supported. Wrist loose. | P, F |
| **7-10** | **BREATH** - Slow inhale nose (4). Slow exhale mouth (6). | T, V (calms vestibular) |
| **10-12** | **EYES** - Soft focus on opponent. See their hips/shoulders. | Q (pre-park) |
| **12-15** | **CUE** - Say your word. "Park." "Push." "Saber." "Trust." | All (trigger) |

**Total: 15 seconds. Every point.**

---

## The Routine by Situation

### Serving
```
0-3s   Feet to line. Platform/pinpoint set. Screw. Arch.
3-5s   Chin tuck. Head still.
5-7s   Grip check. Continental. 3/10. Toss hand ready.
7-10s  Breath. 4 in, 6 out. Visualize toss contact zone.
10-12s Eyes on toss target. See ball leave hand.
12-15s "PARK." (or "Trust.")
```
**Then:** Toss → Chain runs automatically.

### Returning
```
0-3s   Split step timing. Wide base. Tripods.
3-5s   Chin over front shoulder. Head still.
5-7s   Grip 3/10. Racket in front. Off-hand on throat.
7-10s  Breath. 4 in, 6 out. See server's toss.
10-12s Eyes track server → JUMP to YOUR contact zone at THEIR contact.
12-15s "PARK."
```
**Then:** React → Chain runs.

### Between Points (After Point Ends)
```
0-3s   Walk to fence/back. Release tension. Shake hands.
3-5s   Review: ONE fact only. "Late contact." "Good serve."
5-7s   Reset: Feet tripod. Head neutral. Hands loose.
7-10s  Breath. 4 in, 6 out. Clear mind.
10-12s Eyes soft on opponent.
12-15s Cue word. "Next."
```

---

## The Cue Words (Pick ONE per Match)

| Stroke | Cue Options | What It Triggers |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **Forehand** | "Push" "Arch-Oblique" "Park-Push" | Chest fires, palm pushes, front leg brakes |
| **Backhand Saber** | "Saber" "Lat-Draw" "Park-Draw" | Lat pulls, edge leads, chest brakes side-on |
| **Backhand Hybrid** | "Push-Guide" "Two Hands" "Park-Sync" | Bottom pushes, top guides, arrive together |
| **Serve** | "Trophy-Brake" "Load-Snap" "Park-Snap" | Load trophy, brake front hip, snap pronation |
| **Return** | "Jump-Park" "React" "Eyes-Hands" | Eyes jump to zone, hands react |
| **Volley** | "Catch-Pulse" "Block" "Step-Push" | Step forward, firm face, 1-inch pulse |
| **All** | "Trust" "Chain" "Process" "Auto" | Full chain, no conscious control |

## When Routine Breaks

| Signal | Immediate Fix |
|--------|---------------|
| **Rushing** | Next point: ADD 2 seconds to breath phase |
| **Tight grip** | Next point: Conscious 2/10 for ready position |
| **Head moving** | Next point: "Chin tuck" mantra during breath |
| **No park** | Next point: Verbal "PARK" at opponent contact |
| **Negative thought** | Next point: Breath + "Process. This shot." |

## Practice the Routine

### Drill: "15-Second Clock"
1. Set timer for 15 seconds
2. Run full routine
3. Stop exactly at beep
4. Rate completeness
5. 20 reps = routine wired

### Drill: "Pressure Routine"
- Play practice points
- Loser does pushups
- **Only routine compliance scored** (not point outcome)
- Winner = best routine execution

### Drill: "Eyes Closed Routine"
- Run routine with eyes closed
- Feel each phase internally
- Opens proprioception, reduces visual dependence
