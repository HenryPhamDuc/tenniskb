## Trophy Position: The Load

- Elbow at shoulder height or slightly above, **90°**
- Racket points **up, edge up** — never back
- Shoulders tilted, hitting shoulder higher than toss shoulder
- Hip rotated, trunk coiled, loading spiral line
- Toss arm extended upward, pointing at ball, counter-balance

**Coach's Note:** The trophy position isn't a pose. It's the SSC load. If you don't feel a stretch in chest, lat, and shoulder at trophy, you're not loaded. No load = no SSC = no power.

---

## Acceleration: The One-Inch Pulse

- Racket drops behind back from gravity + trunk rotation — **never muscle it down**
- Front leg/hip brakes hard, torso stops
- Arm whips: shoulder IR + elbow extension + **pronation**
- Contact: slightly in front, to the right, above head. **Pronation peaks EXACTLY at contact.**
- Feel: 1-inch pulse. Never a long swing — brake, then snap.

## Serve Types: Same Chain, Different Face

| Serve | Toss Position | Racket Path | Face at Contact | Key Feeling |
|-------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Flat** | Slightly right, inside baseline | Straight up at 45° | Perpendicular to target | Hit through back of ball |
| **Slice** | Further right, wider | Outside-in across ball | Open 10-15°, carving | Brush outside of ball |
| **Kick** | Above head, slightly left | Low to high, 7→1 o'clock | Closed, brushing up | Brush up back of ball |

**Same trophy. Same chain. Only toss and path change.**

## Fault Signals

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Long | Toss low/behind, face opens | Toss higher & inside, close face |
| Net | Toss high/left, face closes | Toss further right, open face |
| Wide | Body opened early, no brake | Front hip brakes, stay side-on |
| No power | No SSC load at trophy | Feel stretch in chest/lat at trophy |
| Shoulder pain | Muscled racket drop | Let gravity + trunk drop racket |

## Pre-Serve Routine (Q-V-ROOT Integration)

1. **Feet to ground:** platform or pinpoint set, tripods active, feel screw
2. **Head to still:** chin tucked, eyes on toss contact zone
3. **Hands to 3/10:** continental, loose wrist, ball in fingertips
4. **Breathe to center:** two slow breaths, vestibular calms
5. **Toss to load:** toss triggers coiling, hips turn, trunk tilts, feel stretch
6. **Trophy to brake to pulse:** see contact point, brake hip, snap pronation

---

## The Serve Checklist

- [ ] Stance: platform or pinpoint, tripods active
- [ ] Grip: continental, loose wrist, 3/10
- [ ] Toss: inside baseline, right of head, peaks 6" above contact
- [ ] Trophy: elbow up, racket edge up, shoulders tilted, feel stretch
- [ ] Brake: front hip/leg stops hard, torso stops
- [ ] Pulse: pronation snaps at contact, 1 inch, never a swing
- [ ] Head: still, eyes on contact, no peek
- [ ] Finish: racket across body, weight forward, balanced
