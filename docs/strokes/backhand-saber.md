# One-Handed Backhand Saber

**Complete Breakdown.**

---

## The Saber Philosophy

> **The backhand isn't a swing. It's a saber draw from the shoulder sheath. The edge leads, and the face squares at the very last instant.**

---

## The Saber CORE Map

| Variable | Saber Implementation |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Q** | Eyes park higher & further side, chin over front shoulder, staying side-on |
| **V** | Head STAYS side-on, chin over front shoulder — never turning to face net |
| **ROOT** | Front foot root holds side-on; back leg pushes, shear snaps lat |
| **8** | Vertical/diagonal 8. High entry behind, crossover drop, up-forward exit |
| **45°** | Diagonal glass — high behind, low in front. Cut down to go up. |
| **BRAKE** | Chest brakes side-on. Lat + tricep snap edge through. |
| **PULSE** | Edge slices down through ball. 1-inch saber pulse. |
| **JIN** | Ting feels lat stretch. Hua absorbs high ball pace. Fa = saber snap. |

---

## Load Phase: As Ball Leaves Opponent

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Eyes** | Soft tracking, head moving, VOR on |
| **Feet** | Front foot steps slightly across. Back hip loads. **Both legs briefly Thực** — wide base, deep root. |
| **Body** | Big unit turn. Non-dominant hand at throat pulls racket back — feel hip→lat→elbow stretch. |
| **Racket** | **High behind you, elbow above shoulder. Edge leads, face slightly closed (points to back fence).** Non-dominant hand pulls; dominant hand rides. |

### Coach's Note

> **The saber backhand loves a high ball. The forehand hates it. Your edge-leading high takeback is exactly the solution for high balls — you start ABOVE the ball and slice downward.**

---

## Park Phase: At the Bounce

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Head** | Stops, but **STAYS SIDE-ON**. Chin over front shoulder — don't turn chin toward net! |
| **Eyes** | Jump to contact zone — further side & higher than forehand, in front of front foot. Lock. Say "park." |
| **Proprioception** | Without looking, feel pinky-side edge leading, lat stretched long? You're loaded. |

---

## Execution: The Saber Draw

| Sequence | What Happens | Feeling |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| **1. Fascial recoil** | Hips start forward, racket **still travels back** — counter-movement loads elastic energy | "Racket still going back as hips go forward" |
| **2. Lat pull** | Lat pulls elbow down & forward, like drawing sword from shoulder sheath | "Elbow drops, edge leads" |
| **3. Feel** | Edge slices down behind ball, face squares at LAST instant | "High to low to high — slice down to go up" |
| **4. Tension** | Upper back firm 5/10, hand 3/10 until contact → 6/10. Wrist firm extension, never floppy. | |
| **5. Brake** | Chest STAYS side-on. Front foot root holds. Lat/tricep snap edge through. | "Chest brakes side-on = saber snaps" |
| **6. Pulse** | Edge slices down through ball, face squares at last instant. 1-inch pulse. | "Short downward chop, 3 inches, then STOP" |

---

## Coach's Note

> **The saber impulse is an even sharper "one-inch punch" than the forehand. Follow through long and you didn't brake — that's just an arm swing, no impulse. The feel is a short downward chop, three inches through the ball, then stop. The racket finishes high, but you stopped the motion.**

## The Saber Quick-Scan Card

**Say before every rally:**

> **GLUTE, LAT, HEAD SIDE-ON, EYES PARK, SABER**

| Cue | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| **GLUTE** | Back hip loads, front foot steps across |
| **LAT** | Non-dominant hand pulls racket back — feel hip-to-lat stretch |
| **HEAD SIDE-ON** | Chin over front shoulder, never turns to net |
| **EYES PARK** | At opponent's contact, eyes jump to contact zone (higher, more lateral) |
| **SABER** | Hips start forward, racket still goes back → lat pulls elbow down, edge slices down, face squares at contact |

---

## Fault Signals

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Balls long | Chest opened early, face opened | Hold side-on longer, chin over shoulder |
| Balls into net | Contact late/low for high ball | Take it earlier, further front, higher elbow |
| No spin/pace | Face opened at contact — edge didn't lead | Higher elbow, edge leads, drop racket behind |
| Shoulder pain | Muscled with arm, no lat stretch | Non-dominant hand pulls back — feel lat stretch |
| Shanks | Chin turned to net, head moved, VOR fired | Chin over shoulder until ball crosses net |

---

## The Saber Checklist

- [ ] Pre-point: wide base, both legs Thực, head side-on, hands 3/10
- [ ] Load: big unit turn, non-dominant hand pulls racket high, feel hip→lat stretch
- [ ] Park: head stops side-on, chin over shoulder, eyes jump to contact zone
- [ ] Execute: hips start forward, racket still drops back (counter-movement), lat pulls elbow down, edge leads
- [ ] Brake: chest brakes side-on, front foot root holds, lat/tric snap edge through
- [ ] Pulse: edge slices down behind ball, face squares at contact, 1-inch pulse
- [ ] Hold: eyes and head stay side-on until ball crosses net
- [ ] If wrist flick or shoulder shrug → lat connection lost. Reset: elbow high.
