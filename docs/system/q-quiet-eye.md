# Q - Quiet Eye

**Where do I park my eyes?**

---

## The Principle

Your eyes must be **parked on the contact zone** at the moment the opponent strikes the ball — and stay there through your own contact.

**Quiet Eye = early fixation, late release.**

---

## The Research

- Elite players fixate **200-300ms before contact**
- Club players fixate **<100ms** or not at all
- **Duration** and **timing** both matter
- **Offset** (when eyes leave) predicts error type

---

## The Protocol

| Phase | Eye Action |
|-------|------------|
| **Opponent's swing** | Soft tracking on ball |
| **At opponent's contact** | **PARK** — jump to your contact zone |
| **Ball flight** | Hold fixation on contact point |
| **Your contact** | Eyes still on contact zone |
| **After contact** | Hold 100-200ms, then release |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | What Happens | Fix |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| **Late park** | Eyes jump at your contact | Park at **opponent's contact** |
| **Early release** | Eyes follow ball to opponent | Hold **through your contact** |
| **Target peek** | Eyes flick to target | Keep **eyes on contact zone** |
| **No park** | Continuous tracking | **Jump** to zone, don't track |

---

## The Drill

**"Park and Hold"**

1. Partner feeds balls
2. Say **"PARK"** aloud at their contact
3. Hold eyes on your contact zone until ball hits strings
4. Count: **"One thousand one, one thousand two"** after contact
5. 20 balls, track success rate

**Goal: 90%+ park rate.**

---

## The Cue

> **"Park at the bounce. Snap at contact."**
