# ROOT - Rooting

**Where does force come from?**

---

## The Principle

**No root = no chain.** Force originates in the ground, travels up the legs, through the torso, out the arm.

**Tripod + Arch + Screw = Root.**

---

## The Three Points

| Point | Location | Feeling |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **1. Big toe mound** | Medial forefoot | Pressure inside |
| **2. Little toe mound** | Lateral forefoot | Pressure outside |
| **3. Heel center** | Posterior | Weight back |

**Triangle = stable base.**

---

## The Screw

| Foot | Direction | Cue |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Right (FH)** | Clockwise | "Screw in" |
| **Left (FH)** | Counter-clockwise | "Screw in" |
| **Both** | Into ground | "Drill the feet" |

---

## The Arch

**Active arch lift** = spiral line tension.

| Arch State | Result |
|------------|--------|
| **Collapsed** | Knee caves, spiral line slack |
| **Passive** | Some tension, leaks at knee |
| **Active lift** | Spiral line taut, knee stable |

---

## The Load Sequence (Forehand)

| Phase | Back Foot | Front Foot |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| **Load** | Thực (screwed in) | Hư (light) |
| **Transfer** | Releasing | Becoming Thực |
| **Brake/Pulse** | Hư (heel up) | Thực (pillar) |

---

## The Drill

**"Screw & Lift"**

1. Barefoot, athletic stance
3. Screw right foot clockwise, feel arch lift
4. Hold 5 seconds, feel spiral line tighten
5. Switch feet, 10 reps each side
6. Add: Shadow forehand, focus on screw → lift → brake

**Goal: Feel the ground → arch → oblique connection.**

---

## The Cue

> **"Screw the feet. Lift the arch. Be the pillar."**
