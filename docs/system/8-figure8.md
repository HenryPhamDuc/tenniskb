# 8 - Figure-8 Regulation

**The Power Regulator.**

---

## The Principle

> **You don't change your swing for power. You change the size and speed of the 8.**

---

## Two 8s, Two Strokes

### Forehand Push: Horizontal 8

```
    Entry (back & out) 
         ↓
    Crossover (drop inside) ← Amortization = here
         ↓
    Exit (push forward) → Brake → Pulse
```

- **Entry:** Racket back & out, chest opens
- **Crossover:** Racket drops inside, butt cap at ball
- **Exit:** Push forward through ball

### Backhand Saber: Vertical/Diagonal 8

```
    Entry (up & back, elbow high)
         ↓
    Crossover (drop behind hip, edge leading)
         ↓
    Exit (edge slices up & forward)
```

- **Entry:** High behind, edge leading
- **Crossover:** Drop behind hip, edge leads
- **Exit:** Edge slices up & forward, chest brakes

---

## Power = 8 Size + Speed

| Power | 8 Size | 8 Speed | Brake | Use Case |
|-------|--------|---------|-------|----------|
| 10% | Tiny (grapefruit) | Slow | Soft | Drop shot |
| 30% | Small (orange) | Slow-med | Gentle | Angle/dink |
| 50% | Medium (basketball) | Medium | Medium | Rally |
| 70% | Med-small | Fast | Firm | Pressure |
| 100% | Small (grapefruit) | **Violent** | **Hard** | Winner |

**Key insight:** 100% is SMALLER than 50% — just faster, harder brake.

---

## The Counter-Intuitive Truth

> **Look at the 100% trace. It's one of the smallest shapes — just the fastest and hardest-braked. Power was never about a bigger backswing.**

---

## The 8 Connects Everything

| Variable | How the 8 Regulates It |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Q** | 8 keeps head still — hand draws 8, head is gimbal |
| **V** | Smooth 8 = no head jerk = quiet VOR |
| **ROOT** | 8 powered by root — feet push, hips turn, hand traces |
| **F** | 8 keeps fabric tensioned — no slack at reversal |
| **P** | You FEEL the 8 size — tiny = touch, violent = power |
| **T** | 8 keeps tent tensioned — no slack = instant transfer |
| **SSC** | **Crossover = Amortization** — keep it smooth <200ms |
| **45°** | 8 lives ON the 45° glass plane |
| **BRAKE** | Exit of 8 = brake point |

---

## The Drill

**"Shadow 8s → Add Brake → Regulate"**

1. 20 continuous figure-8s, hand only, butt cap leading
2. No pause anywhere in the path
3. Add front-leg brake at exit
4. Partner calls "20%, 50%, 100%" — same path, change size/speed
5. **If you pull racket way back for 100%, you've lost it — big takeback = slow 8**

---

## The Cue

> **"Small 8, hard brake. One-inch pulse. Power was never a bigger backswing."**
