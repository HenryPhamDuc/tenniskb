# JIN / KINH - Ting, Hua, Fa

**The Three Manifestations.**

---

## The Principle

> **Tensegrity is the structure. SSC is the engine. Jin is what comes out the other end.**

Jin isn't muscular power. It's **whole-body elastic force transmitted through a tensegrity structure.**

---

## The Three Types

| Type | Vietnamese | Chinese | What It Is | Tennis Application |
|------|------------|---------|------------|-------------------|
| **Ting Jin** | Thính Kình | 聽勁 | **Listening** — feel tension in your fascial lines, feel the ball at contact | 4ms dwell = feel face angle, spin, pressure through palm |
| **Hua Jin** | Hóa Kình | 化勁 | **Neutralizing** — absorb & redirect incoming force | Short takeback on fast ball = absorb pace, redirect |
| **Fa Jin** | Phát Kình | 發勁 | **Explosive** — snap, no wind-up pause | Push = Fa forward. Saber = Fa down & forward |

---

## The Cycle

```
Ting → Hua → Fa → (next ball) → Ting → Hua → Fa...
```

Every shot contains all three. The cycle repeats ball after ball.

---

## 1. Ting Jin — Listening

| What | How |
|------|-----|
| **Feel the ball** | 4ms dwell on strings through palm fascia |
| **Feel your stretch** | Spiral/back line tension building |
| **Eyes closed shadow** | Train spindles to tolerate stretch |

> **If you can't feel face angle without looking, your proprioception has been leaning on vision — and that kills Quiet Eye.**

---

## 2. Hua Jin — Neutralizing

| What | How |
|------|-----|
| **Absorb pace** | Short takeback on fast ball = don't add force, transform it |
| **Requires Q + V** | Still head, quiet eyes = no fight, just redirect |
| **Pro secret** | They never fight pace. They redirect it. |

---

## 3. Fa Jin — Explosive Release

| What | How |
|------|-----|
| **The snap** | Stretch → immediate release, no pause |
| **Why no pause matters** | Elastic energy dissipates as heat in ~1 second |
| **Push** | Fa Jin traveling forward |
| **Saber** | Fa Jin traveling down & forward |

---

## The Three Jin in Action

| Shot | Ting (Feel) | Hua (Absorb) | Fa (Release) |
|------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Serve return (fast)** | Ball speed/spin in 50ms | Short takeback absorbs pace | Block/punch, firm wrist, redirect |
| **Rally forehand** | Spiral line stretch | Let ball come to zone | Palm push through |
| **High ball attack** | Lat stretch behind | Drop in, absorb | Draw saber, edge pulse |
| **Volley** | Contact zone in 2ms | Firm face absorbs | Pulse forward |
| **Drop shot** | Face open ~30° | Absorb ALL pace | Tiny Fa — pure touch |

---

## The Rule

> **Fa Jin alone, without Ting to feel and Hua to absorb, just makes you a ball-basher. Not a Jin player.**

---

## The Checklist

| Before Swing | At Bounce | At Contact |
|--------------|-----------|------------|
| Feel fascial stretch (Ting) | Absorb incoming energy (Hua) | Snap — no pause (Fa) |

---

## The Drill

| Drill | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Eyes-closed shadow** | Ting — feel every inch of stretch |
| **Absorb & redirect** | Hua — partner hits fast, you block/redirect |
| **Bounce-drop-push** | Fa — racket moving forward before ball peaks |

---

## The Cue

> **TING: Feel the stretch. HUA: Absorb and redirect. FA: Release with no pause.**
