# V - Vestibular

**Why does my head move?**

---

## The Principle

A **still head** = quiet vestibular system = stable face angle.

Move the head → VOR fires → face twists → ball goes sideways.

---

## The Reflex

**VOR (Vestibulo-Ocular Reflex):**
- Head rotates → eyes counter-rotate to stabilize gaze
- When VOR fires during swing, it pulls the face open/closed
- **Still head = VOR quiet = face stable**

---

## The Protocol

| Checkpoint | Head Position |
|------------|---------------|
| **Ready** | Chin slightly tucked, eyes level |
| **Takeback** | Head still, chin over front shoulder |
| **At bounce** | Eyes park, head frozen |
| **Swing** | Head stays in space |
| **Contact** | Eyes on contact zone, head still |
| **After contact** | Hold 100ms, then release |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | What Happens | Fix |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| **Peeking** | Head turns to target | "Chin over shoulder" |
| **Head lift** | Face opens, ball goes long | "Chin tucked through contact" |
| **Head dip** | Face closes, ball nets | "Eyes level, chin up slightly" |
| **Late freeze** | VOR fires mid-swing | Freeze **at opponent's contact** |

---

## The Drill

**"Chin Tuck & Freeze"**

1. Shadow swing without ball
2. Chin slightly tucked, eyes on imaginary contact point
3. Freeze at contact — hold 3 seconds
3. Check: Did head move? Chin tucked?
4. 20 reps, then add ball

**Goal: Zero head movement from bounce to contact.**

---

## The Cue

> **"Chin tucked. Eyes parked. Head frozen."**
