# SSC - Stretch-Shortening Cycle

**The Engine.**

---

## The Three Phases

| Phase | Name | Duration | Key |
|-------|------|----------|-----|
| **1** | Eccentric | Variable | Load |
| **2** | Amortization | **< 200ms** | Switch |
| **3** | Concentric | Explosive | Release |

---

## The Rule

> **Pause > 200ms = Energy lost as heat.**
> **Smooth < 200ms = Free power.**

---

## The Three SSC Chains

### Forehand Push (3 in series)

| SSC # | Location | Eccentric Load | Concentric Release |
|-------|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Legs | Knee bend → extend | Ground force up |
| 2 | Torso | Chest coils → uncoils | Torso whip |
| 3 | Forearm | Wrist lays back → snaps | Wrist snap |

### Backhand Saber (2 in series)

| SSC # | Location | Eccentric Load | Concentric Release |
|-------|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Lat/Back Line | High elbow → stretch | Lat pulls elbow down |
| 2 | Tricep/Fascia | Elbow flex → extend | Edge snaps through |

---

## The Amortization Curve

```
Energy Retained
100% |●
  70% |  ●
  30% |    ●
   0% |______●______
       0   100  200  300ms
```

| Amortization | Energy | Feel |
|--------------|--------|------|
| 0ms (continuous) | 100% | Effortless whip |
| 50-100ms | ~70% | Good, slight loss |
| 150-200ms | ~30% | Muscle doing work |
| >200ms (pause) | ~0% | Heavy, muscled |

---

## The Rule

> **Stretch flows directly into shorten. No pause. The crossover of the Figure-8 IS the amortization phase.**

---

## The Drill

**"Bounce-Drop-Push"**

1. Drop ball, let it bounce
2. **Constraint:** Racket moving forward BEFORE ball reaches peak
3. Forces short amortization
4. Wait for peak → you've paused → no SSC

**20 reps. Feel the continuous stretch-snap.**

---

## The Cue

> **"Stretch. No pause. Snap."**
