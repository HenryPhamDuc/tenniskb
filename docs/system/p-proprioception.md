# P - Proprioception

**Can I feel the stretch?**

---

## The Principle

**4 milliseconds.** That's the ball dwell time on strings.

Your palm fascia feels: face angle, spin, pressure, impact location — **in 4ms.**

No vision. Pure feel.

---

## The Receptors

| Receptor | Location | Detects | Timing |
|----------|----------|---------|--------|
| **Ruffini** | Joint capsules, skin | Stretch, joint angle | Continuous |
| **Pacinian** | Deep fascia, joints | Vibration, rapid change | <10ms |
| **Muscle spindle** | Muscle belly | Length, velocity | 20-50ms |
| **Golgi tendon** | Tendon junction | Force, tension | 50-100ms |

---

## The Feeling Scale

| Tension | Feeling | Use |
|---------|---------|-----|
| **1-2/10** | Barely there | Ready position |
| **3/10** | Aware of racket | Takeback, drop |
| **4-5/10** | Clear stretch | Loading phase |
| **6-7/10** | Strong stretch | Pre-contact |
| **6-7/10** | Firm at contact | **Contact (30ms)** |
| **3/10** | Release | Follow-through |

---

## The Feeling Checkpoints

| Phase | Feel For |
|-------|----------|
| **Ready** | Racket weight in palm, face angle |
| **Takeback** | Oblique stretch, spiral line loading |
| **Drop** | Wrist lays back, forearm stretch |
| **Swing** | Chest pull, oblique fire |
| **Contact** | **Palm pressure spike (6-7/10)** |
| **Release** | Instant drop to 3/10 |

---

## The Drill

**"Eyes Closed Contact"**

1. Partner feeds easy balls
2. Close eyes at bounce
3. Track purely by feel: where's the face? where's the ball?
3. Call out: "Forehand/Backhand, Clean/Off-center, Face open/closed"
4. 20 balls, track accuracy

**Goal: 80%+ correct feel calls.**

---

## The Cue

> **"Feel the face. Feel the stretch. Feel the spike."**
