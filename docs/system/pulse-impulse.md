# PULSE - Impulse / Fa Jin

**The 1-Inch Pulse.**

---

## The Principle

> **The pulse IS the release. Brake stops the body. Pulse releases the racket.**

---

## The Physics

```
Impulse = ∫ Force dt = Change in Momentum
```

- **Brake** = sudden deceleration of proximal segments
- **Pulse** = proximal-to-distal energy transfer
- **Contact** = ~4ms dwell time
- **Peak force** = 30ms after contact initiation

---

## The Two Pulses

| Stroke | Action | Distance | Peak Force Timing |
|--------|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Forehand Push** | Palm pushes THROUGH | 1 inch | 30ms post-contact |
| **Saber Backhand** | Edge slices THROUGH | 3 inches | 30ms post-contact |

---

## The Feeling

| Stroke | Cue |
|--------|-----|
| **Forehand** | "Palm pushes one inch through the ball" |
| **Backhand** | "Edge slices three inches through the ball" |

**Not a swing. A push. A slice. A pulse.**

---

## The Drills

### 1. Wall Pulse (Forehand)
1. 1 inch from wall, racket butt touching
2. No takeback — palm behind racket
3. Push wall, feel spiral line fire
4. Instant release
5. 20 reps

### 2. Saber Pulse (Backhand)
1. Edge leading, racket high behind
2. Drop from gravity, lat pulls elbow down
3. Edge snaps through
4. Freeze at contact + 1 inch
5. 20 reps

### 3. Regulation Drill
Partner calls: "20%, 50%, 100%"
- **20%**: Tiny pulse, soft brake
- **50%**: Medium pulse, firm brake
- **100%**: Violent pulse, violent brake
- **Same tiny takeback every time**

---

## The Cue

> **"Brake hard. Pulse one inch. The racket does the rest."**
