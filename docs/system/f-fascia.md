# F - Fascia

**How does force travel?**

---

## The Principle

**Fascia is the transmission lines.** Muscles contract; fascia transmits.

Two primary lines for tennis:

| Line | Stroke | Path |
|------|--------|------|
| **Spiral Line** | Forehand Push | Foot → hip → oblique → shoulder → arm |
| **Back Line (Superficial)** | Backhand Saber | Heel → hamstring → sacrum → lat → arm |

---

## The Feeling

| Line | Load Cue | Release Cue |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Spiral** | "Stretch the X across the belly" | "Chest fires, arm whips" |
| **Back** | "Stretch from heel to lat" | "Lat pulls elbow down" |

---

## The Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Continuous tension** | Slack = energy leak |
| **No local knots** | Tight forearm = blocked line |
| **Load before fire** | Stretch before shortening (SSC) |
| **Brake to release** | Front leg stops → line snaps |

---

## Common Breaks

| Break | Symptom | Fix |
|-------|---------|-----|
| **Forearm tight** | Spiral line blocked at wrist | 3/10 grip, loose wrist |
| **Shoulder hike** | Back line blocked at trap | Lat pulls down, chest open |
| **Torso collapse** | Lines buckle at waist | Root + Tensegrity |
| **Early arm** | Lines not loaded | Wait for hip lead |

---

## The Drill

**"Line Trace"**

1. Slow shadow forehand
2. Trace Spiral Line with opposite hand: foot → hip → ribs → shoulder → hand
3. Feel continuous tension, no slack
4. Shadow backhand, trace Back Line: heel → hamstring → lat → hand
5. 10 reps each, then hit balls

**Goal: Feel the line, not the muscle.**

---

## The Cue

> **"Two lines. One continuous. No knots."**
