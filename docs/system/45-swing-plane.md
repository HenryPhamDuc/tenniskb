# 45° - Swing Plane

**The Sweet Spot.**

---

## The Principle

> **Not vertical. Not horizontal. Forty-five degrees is where drive and topspin become a single force.**

---

## Why 45°

| Plane | Drive | Spin | Margin | Result |
|-------|-------|------|--------|--------|
| **0° (Flat)** | High | None | None | Fast, no margin, goes long |
| **45°** | **High** | **High** | **High** | **Heavy, penetrating, kicks** |
| **90° (Vertical)** | None | Maximum | Maximum | Moonball, sits up |

**At 45°, you split energy 50/50.** For court geometry, that's optimal — clears net by ~3ft, still dips inside baseline.

---

## The 45° Glass Plane

Imagine a **sheet of glass at 45°** from behind you, through contact, to target.

- **Racket lives ON the glass**
- **Figure-8 traced ON the glass**
- **Brake happens ON the glass**
- **Pulse pushes THROUGH the glass**

---

## Brake on 45°

| Stroke | Brake Direction | Feeling |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| **Forehand** | Front leg brakes **forward AND up at 45°** | Screw into ground, push up |
| **Backhand** | Chest brakes side-on, **lat brakes UP at 45°** | Elbow stops rising, edge snaps |

**Bruce Lee's one-inch punch:** Fist travels slightly UP, never straight across. That's the 45° lift.

---

## Self-Check: Are You on 45°?

| Check | Too Flat (0°) | 45° (Sweet) | Too Vertical (90°) |
|-------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Video (side)** | Hand flat across chest | **Back pocket → opposite shoulder** | Hand straight up over shoulder |
| **Ball flight** | No dip, goes long | **Rises ~3ft over net, dips sharp** | High arc, no penetration |
| **Body feel** | Only chest push | **Chest push + lat lift together** | Only shoulder shrug |
| **Contact sound** | Thin slap | **Heavy thud + hiss** | Soft brush |

---

## The Rule

> **45° was never a swing thought. It's the emergent result of: Push/Saber feel + Figure-8 on the plane + Hard brake on the plane. You don't "swing at 45°." You push/saber on the glass, and 45° happens.**

---

## The Drill

**"Back Pocket to Far Shoulder"**

1. Shadow forehand, film from side
2. Check: hand path from back pocket → opposite shoulder
3. If flat across chest → too horizontal
4. If straight up over shoulder → too vertical
5. **Diagonal = 45°**

---

## The Cue

> **"Back pocket to far shoulder. Chest push + lat lift. Brake at 45°. Pulse through the glass."**
